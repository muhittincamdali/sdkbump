         Fix: [B] Find the window through the scene

How to fix

[A]      Use the SwiftUI scene API
         1. A SwiftUI App always runs on scenes, so UIKit never calls this app delegate method. Use
            the SwiftUI replacement named in the finding on a view in your WindowGroup.
         https://github.com/muhittincamdali/sdkbump/blob/main/docs/guides/native-ios.md

[B]      Find the window through the scene
         1. From a view or view controller, use view.window or view.window?.windowScene.
         2. Elsewhere, pick the foreground UIWindowScene from UIApplication.shared.connectedScenes
            and use its keyWindow or windows.
         https://github.com/muhittincamdali/sdkbump/blob/main/docs/guides/native-ios.md
